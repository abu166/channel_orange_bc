# VetoHealth: product context

**One user, one pain.** Maya, 31, US, spends about $80 a month on skincare and supplements chosen from TikTok and Reels. She can't tell which health claims are real, waits about 34.5 days for a dermatologist, and nobody explains her blood test. Money leaks on guesses.

**What we sell.** A free evidence check for any health claim (live PubMed, studies that disagree shown too), a free routine checker, then paid clinician services: $10 written lab review, $20 lab video consult, $129 home skin check, and memberships (Lab Plus $9/mo, Skin Plus $29/mo, Whole Health $34/mo). Prices live in `lib/pricing.ts`.

**Mode by surface (Impeccable).** Landing and Plans = *Persuade*. Check a claim, Routine, Labs, Visit, History = *Operate*. Reports = *Read*.

**Voice.** Plain, calm, specific. Say what the studies found and what to do next. No hype, no superlatives, no "not X, Y" slogans, no emoji, few dashes. Never diagnose or prescribe. Label illustrative values as illustrative.

**Must feel like.** A trustworthy health newsroom that takes your side: confident type, white space, one brand red used sparingly, colour that always means something.
